# Secure sign-up and accounts

- **Shipped:** 2026-10-27 · **Published:** —
- **For:** Partners
- **PR:** #7, #8, #9

## In one line
Safer accounts: email verification, password reset, and sign-out on every device.

## Details
- New accounts confirm their email with a 6-digit code, which blocks fake and bot sign-ups.
- Forgot-password reset by email.
- "Sign out on all devices" with one click.
- Protection against repeated sign-in attempts.
