# Smoother booking for partners

- **Shipped:** 2026-11-01 · **Published:** —
- **For:** Partners, Players
- **PR:** #29

## In one line
A set of fixes that make daily front-desk work faster and stop bookings and payments from slipping through the cracks.

## Highlights
- **Reschedule** a booking (date, time or court) or fix customer details, with double-booking prevented.
- **Customers can cancel** their own booking online, and staff are notified.
- **Payments:** customers can add the amount paid and a receipt screenshot. Staff can mark a payment as not found.
- **Faster desk work:** click an empty hour to book it, search by name or phone, **Approve all**, **Paid in full**, **Mark no-show**.
- **Scheduling:** weekly closed days, holiday and maintenance closures, peak-hour pricing, and how far ahead people can book.

## Talking points
- Fewer lost bookings and fewer payment mix-ups.
- Peak-hour pricing helps venues earn more at busy times.
