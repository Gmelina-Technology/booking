# Important notices for partners

- **Shipped:** 2026-11-02 · **Published:** —
- **For:** Partners
- **PR:** #37

## In one line
The CourtHub team can post notices at the top of the partner dashboard so partners don't miss important updates.

## How it works
- Information notices can be dismissed with **Got it**.
- Some notices ask for a response, like agreeing to updated fee terms or replying to the team. They stay until the partner responds.

## Talking points
- Partners stay up to date without digging through emails.
- Mostly internal, so it's better as a "how we keep you informed" mention than a headline feature.
