# Your own booking page

- **Shipped:** 2026-10-22 · **Published:** —
- **For:** Partners
- **PR:** #3, #4

## In one line
Every venue signs up and gets its own public booking page at `/p/@yourname`, with a banner it designs itself.

## Details
- Self sign-up, with your own handle and page.
- Banner editor with four templates (Collage, Spotlight, Split, Minimal), your own colours, text, buttons and up to 4 photos, with a live desktop and phone preview.
- Emails to customers carry the venue's own name and contact details.

## Talking points
- A professional booking website in minutes, with no web designer needed.
- Make it look like your brand, not ours.
