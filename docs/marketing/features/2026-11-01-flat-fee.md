# Flat fee per booking

- **Shipped:** 2026-11-01 · **Published:** —
- **For:** Partners
- **PR:** #32

## In one line
Partners pay one simple flat fee per completed booking, however long it runs.

## Details
- No monthly fee, and nothing to pay on quiet days.
- Fees for bookings before the change are unaffected.
- Partners can see their current rate and any upcoming change on the **Billing** page.

## Talking points
- Predictable and easy to explain: one booking, one fee.
- Longer bookings don't cost the partner more.
