# Find courts near you

- **Shipped:** 2026-11-02 · **Published:** —
- **For:** Players, Partners
- **PR:** #44

## In one line
Players can find courts near them on a map, and every venue has its own place in a searchable directory.

## For players
- A new **Venues** page: search, tap **Near me** to sort by distance ("2.3 km away"), and switch between a list and a map.
- The home page shows **Popular venues**, the three most-booked.

## For partners
- Pin your venue on the map from your address, then drag the pin to the exact spot.
- Paused pages are hidden from the directory automatically.

## Talking points
- Get discovered by players who are nearby and ready to play.
- Popular venues get extra visibility on the home page.
