# Bookings calendar

- **Shipped:** 2026-10-22 · **Published:** —
- **For:** Partners
- **PR:** #5

## In one line
See every booking on a calendar by month, week or day, and add walk-in or phone bookings in seconds.

## Details
- Colour-coded bookings: new request, awaiting payment, confirmed.
- The day view shows one column per court, and it works on phones too.
- Click any booking to approve it, verify payment or cancel.
- **Create booking** for walk-ins and phone calls, with double-booking prevented.

## Talking points
- Your whole venue at a glance.
- Online and walk-in bookings in one place, so no more paper logbook.
