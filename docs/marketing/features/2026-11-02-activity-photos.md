# Background photos for activities

- **Shipped:** 2026-11-02 · **Published:** —
- **For:** Partners
- **PR:** #41

## In one line
Partners can give each activity (basketball, badminton, pickleball…) its own photo.

## Where it shows
- The activity cards on the venue page.
- The header of the activity's own page.
- The staff activities list.

## Talking points
- Show off your real courts. Photos sell better than icons.
- Easy to add or remove from the activity editor, with a live preview.
