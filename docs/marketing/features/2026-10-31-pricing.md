# Partner pricing: we grow when you grow

- **Shipped:** 2026-10-31 · **Published:** —
- **For:** Partners
- **PR:** #12

## In one line
No monthly fee. CourtHub only earns a small fee on bookings that actually happen.

## Details
- A public **Pricing** page with the "We only grow when your courts are full" message.
- A **Billing** page for partners: their current rate, a monthly statement and totals.
- New partners agree to the fee terms at sign-up.

## Talking points
- Zero risk to start: quiet months cost nothing.
- Note: the public pricing page deliberately doesn't name a number or say "free". Keep marketing copy consistent with that.

_Later changed to a flat fee per booking, see [flat fee](2026-11-01-flat-fee.md)._
