# Optional downpayment

- **Shipped:** 2026-11-01 · **Published:** —
- **For:** Partners, Players
- **PR:** #27

## In one line
Partners choose, activity by activity, whether players have to pay a downpayment to book.

## How it works
- Turned off: the booking is confirmed when approved, and the player pays at the front desk.
- Turned on (the default): it works as before.

## Talking points
- Ask for a downpayment for big courts and skip it for quick, cheap bookings like table hire.
- Less friction for players means more bookings.
