# Open play, workshops and tournaments

- **Shipped:** 2026-11-05 · **Published:** —
- **For:** Partners, Players
- **PR:** #47

## In one line
Venues can host open play, workshops and tournaments that players find, register for and pay for on the venue's page.

## For partners
- Create an event: type, sport, date and time, courts, number of spots (players, or teams for tournaments), price, and whether players pay online or at the venue.
- Save it as a draft or publish it. Publishing reserves the courts, so nobody can book over the event.
- See who's registered, who's paid and how much the event has brought in.
- Duplicate an event to run it again. Cancelling emails every registrant and frees the courts.

## For players
- An **Upcoming events** section on each venue's page, with a "spots left" bar.
- Register yourself, or name your team and players for a tournament.
- Pay online by QR or at the venue. You can cancel up until registration closes.

## Talking points
- Fill quiet hours with group sessions instead of single bookings.
- One place to promote, fill and get paid for events. No spreadsheets or group chats.
- It can't overbook: the last spot only goes to one person.
