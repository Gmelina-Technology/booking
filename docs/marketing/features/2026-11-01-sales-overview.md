# Sales overview

- **Shipped:** 2026-11-01 · **Published:** —
- **For:** Partners
- **PR:** #26

## In one line
A Sales page that shows partners how their business is growing.

## What partners see
- This month's revenue, bookings, average booking and new vs. returning customers, compared with last month.
- A 12-month revenue chart, with the best month and recent trend.
- Revenue by activity, plus a month-by-month table.
- A **Sales this year** chart on the dashboard home.

## Talking points
- Know which sports and months make you money.
- Track returning customers, not just bookings.
