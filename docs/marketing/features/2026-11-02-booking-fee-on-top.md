# Customers pay the booking fee

- **Shipped:** 2026-11-02 · **Published:** —
- **For:** Partners, Players
- **PR:** #42

## In one line
The small booking fee is added to the customer's total, so partners keep their full court rate.

## How it looks
- The booking form shows the breakdown, for example "₱300 + ₱15 booking fee = ₱315".
- The customer's booking page and emails show the price, booking fee and total.
- Walk-in and phone bookings made by staff don't add the fee.

## Talking points
- Your court rate is your take-home rate.
- Customers see the fee clearly before they book. No surprises.
