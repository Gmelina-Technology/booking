# Link your social media and share your page

- **Shipped:** 2026-11-01 · **Published:** —
- **For:** Partners
- **PR:** #28

## In one line
Venues can show their Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and X accounts, and shared links get a good-looking preview card.

## Details
- Social icons on the venue page.
- When a link is shared, the preview shows the venue name, sports and a "Book a court online" button in the venue's colours.
- A share menu: Facebook, X, WhatsApp, email, or copy link (the phone's share sheet on mobile).

## Talking points
- Every share is an advert for your venue.
- Connect your booking page and your social following in one click.
