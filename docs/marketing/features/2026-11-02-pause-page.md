# Pause your page

- **Shipped:** 2026-11-02 · **Published:** —
- **For:** Partners, Players
- **PR:** #36

## In one line
Closed for a holiday or renovation? Pause new bookings and reopen automatically on the date you choose.

## How it works
- From **Business profile → Page status**, add an optional message for customers and a reopening date.
- Your page stays online and shows a friendly "not taking bookings right now" notice with your message and contact details.
- Existing bookings aren't affected. Resume any time, or let it reopen on its own.

## Talking points
- No more turning customers away by phone. The page explains it for you.
- Set it once and forget it, because it reopens by itself.
