# Account management and privacy

- **Shipped:** 2026-10-30 · **Published:** —
- **For:** Partners, Players
- **PR:** #10

## In one line
Partners control their own account, and customer data is handled responsibly.

## Details
- Change your password or sign-in email, or delete your account, all from the **Account** page.
- Privacy and Terms pages written with the Philippine Data Privacy Act in mind.
- Staff can erase a customer's personal details from old bookings.
- Booking requests nobody reviews in time are declined automatically, and the customer is told.

## Talking points
- Customers never wait forever for a reply.
- Built with data privacy in mind.
